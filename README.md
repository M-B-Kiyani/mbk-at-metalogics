potential MetaLogics clients

potential strategic partners

CORE BRAND POSITION

The website should establish MBK as:

Technology & AI Systems Lead

with:

Electrical & Telecommunications Engineering

as the professional foundation.

The site should communicate an engineer's approach to technology:

understand the system → identify the real constraint → diagnose the cause → design the appropriate solution → make it reliable

The differentiation is NOT:

"I know lots of technologies."

The differentiation is:

I understand how technology behaves when it meets real systems, real data, real users, and real operational constraints.

This should be communicated subtly through the design and content.

BRAND RELATIONSHIP

MBK is the personal identity.

MetaLogics is the organization/ecosystem through which broader services and projects are delivered.

Do NOT portray MBK as Founder/CEO unless explicitly stated in the supplied specification.

Do NOT invent formal management titles.

Do NOT make the personal page compete with MetaLogics.

MetaLogics should appear as a credible next step:

Learn about MBK → understand how he works → discover MetaLogics → explore services/projects

VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a premium executive technology aesthetic.

Think:

engineering consultancy

technology leadership profile

modern corporate architecture

high-end digital consultancy

Do NOT make it look like:

a SaaS landing page

a generic developer portfolio

an AI startup template

cyberpunk

gaming UI

Web3

crypto

neon AI

excessive glassmorphism

excessive gradients

giant 3D AI brains

stock business photography

template-generated design

Use the MetaLogics visual language as the brand foundation.

If the exact MetaLogics color values are available through supplied assets or accessible project information, use them.

If they are NOT available, create a restrained corporate palette that is clearly compatible with MetaLogics rather than inventing a flashy personal palette.

The design should feel like a premium personal layer of the MetaLogics brand.

CTA

→ systems thinking
→ infrastructure
→ reliability
→ diagnosis
→ technology solutions

DESIGN QUALITY BAR

Before considering the build complete, critically inspect the result.

Ask:

Does this look like an executive technology profile?

Does it look credible?

Does MBK feel like a real person rather than an AI-generated persona?

Is engineering visible without overpowering AI/technology?

Is the MetaLogics relationship obvious but subordinate?

Can a CEO understand what MBK actually does?

Does the site avoid generic developer-portfolio patterns?

Does it avoid generic AI visual clichés?

Are capabilities expressed as outcomes rather than a skill dump?

Does the page have a clear narrative?

Does the visual hierarchy feel intentional?

Does mobile retain the same quality as desktop?

If any answer is no, improve the implementation before stopping.

IMPORTANT BUILD RULE

Do not stop after creating a rough skeleton.

Build the actual polished interface.

Do not fill sections with generic placeholder copy merely to make the page look complete.

Use the supplied Claude specification as the content authority.

Where content is missing, make the layout ready for that content without inventing facts.

Do not turn every section into cards.

Use composition, typography, whitespace, hierarchy, and selective visual elements to create the executive feel.

The final result should feel like a high-end personal technology leadership profile belonging to someone inside a serious technology solutions agency — not a template portfolio.
